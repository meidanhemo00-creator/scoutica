Stav Farache

Founder & CEO

A perspective shaped by venture and operations.

Stav Farache founded Scoutica to help US investors approach Israel’s technology ecosystem with more context and closer relationships.

Most recently, she led field test operations at Covenant, an Israeli defense technology startup, overseeing team readiness, vendor coordination, logistics, and budgets.

Before that, she worked in venture capital operations at 10D, supporting the firm’s CEOs across executive operations, vendor management, and budgeting. She served as a commissioned officer in Israeli defense technology and intelligence environments, managing software, hardware, and cybersecurity projects.

That combination shapes how Scoutica works: familiarity with how Israeli ventures operate day to day, relationships across the ecosystem, and an understanding of the context US investors need.
