Stav Farache

Founder & CEO

A perspective shaped by venture and operations.

Stav Farache founded Scoutica to help US investors approach Israel’s technology ecosystem with more context and closer relationships. Her background spans venture capital operations, defense technology, and officer service in Israeli defense and intelligence environments.

Stav served as a commissioned officer in Israeli defense technology and intelligence environments. She completed officer training, led and mentored a team, and managed software, hardware, and cybersecurity projects. The service gave her the discipline and the relationships that Scoutica draws on.

At 10D, Stav worked in venture capital operations and executive support to the firm’s CEOs. Her work covered executive operations, vendor management, budgeting, and invoicing at an active venture capital firm, which gave her a close view of how a fund runs day to day.

At Covenant, an Israeli defense technology startup, Stav ran field test operations from end to end. That meant getting the team ready, coordinating vendors, handling logistics and procurement, and managing the budget. It gave her working knowledge of how defense technology companies are built, funded, and scaled.

Stav has built and managed CRM and value creation processes. At Scoutica the same discipline keeps every relationship, company, and follow-up organized from first contact to introduction.

That combination shapes how Scoutica works: familiarity with how Israeli companies operate day to day, relationships across the ecosystem, and an understanding of the context US investors need.
